Reccomended Safe Games

It should be noted that any gacha game can be played unsafely, and any gacha game can (technically) be played safely. These are just some games that I know from experience don’t require spending money to progress through and enjoy.

Umamusume: Pretty Derby

Also known as “the horse girl racing game,” Umamusume is directed towards tween girls. In my experience, it’s completely playable without spending a cent, though there is a separate “paid” currency that may make children feel like they need to buy it. Casual players will be fine being completely F2P, though it may be difficult to compete with the more competitive players. For kids, though, who ideally should only be playing gacha games in a casual, non-consuming way, this is a good option.

Reverse: 1999

Age rating is more for older teens, about PG-13. This is for history fans that love complex sci-fi, well-written female characters, and diverse representations of queerness (which are difficult to find in most gacha games). The gacha isn’t the most forgiving, but each version gives plenty of rewards, and I’ve progressed through the story fine without paying. Rather than gacha, R1999’s monetization focuses more on optional “skins” and outfits for characters, which are a set, one-time price.

Honkai: Star Rail

I am admittedly biased because I’ve been playing this game for three years and adore it, but I find HSR incredibly F2P-friendly. The gacha is fairly generous, the rewards are generous, and many events grant free high-rarity characters. A couple endgame modes even let you use characters you don’t own, dampening the FOMO players may feel for not otherwise being able to use popular characters. There is also no direct player-versus-player content, which means less competitiveness that drives spending.

Other games I've heard are very friendly (but can't personally vouch for) include Arknights, Path to Nowhere, Zenless Zone Zero, and Girls' Frontline.
