The Free Perspective

“Free-to-play,” or F2P players, are players of gacha games who (broadly) do not spend money on the gacha system. There are various degrees of F2P. A player might occasionally buy a battle pass (a month of extra rewards) or an optional outfit, or they may never spend any money at all. For our purpose, let's assume “F2P” means truly free: no money is being spent.

Gacha games appeal to F2P players because they are, at their core, free. Free to download and free to play. A game as long and high-quality as Honkai: Star Rail could easily go for $100 or more without the gacha model, but with it, there is no upfront cost. Players without the financial freedom to buy an expensive game have access to games with a significant budget.

However, not all gachas are made equal. While games like Arknights are known for not needing high-rarity units to progress in the story and play the game well, others, such as AFK Journey, are nearly impossible to do well in without paying money. These games are known as “play-to-win,” or P2W. No matter how technically good a F2P player is, and no matter how incredible their luck is, these games are dominated by whoever can spend the most money.

F2P players may not fund the production of these games directly, but between external purchases (such as merchandise) and publicity, they are an important part of the gaming community. Gacha games should be viable to play well regardless of how much is spent.
